WHAT THE TWO WORDS MEAN
-----------------------
p   = the policy for malmotvatt.com itself
sp  = the policy for any subdomain of it (news.malmotvatt.com,
      new.malmotvatt.com, and so on)

  none        = "report fakes to me, but deliver them anyway"  (no protection)
  quarantine  = "put fakes in the recipient's spam folder"

sp is worth changing too. If you set p only, your main domain is protected
but anyone can still forge mail from anything.malmotvatt.com and it lands in
the inbox. Nothing of yours sends mail from a subdomain - the website forms
send as info@malmotvatt.com, and Fortnox sends under fortnox.se - so there is
nothing that can break.


THE REST OF THE LINE, IN CASE YOU WONDER WHAT IT IS
---------------------------------------------------
  adkim=r   relaxed DKIM alignment - the normal setting
  aspf=r    relaxed SPF alignment - the normal setting
  pct=100   apply the policy to 100% of mail
  fo=1      send a forensic report if either check fails
  rf=afrf   the report format
  ri=86400  send the summary report once every 24 hours
  rua=      where the daily summary reports go
  ruf=      where the forensic reports go

All of these are defaults or were already in your old record. None of them
need touching.


BEFORE YOU SAVE - THE DUPLICATE

you save the change. Two records that say different things = DMARC ignores
both, and you end up with no policy at all while everything still looks fine.
